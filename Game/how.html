How to Play

Imani’s Rock Paper Scissor Game is simple, fast, and fun! Follow these steps to play:

1. Choose Your Move

Type in your choice: rock, paper, or scissors. Make sure you spell your move correctly, then press Submit.

2. Computer’s Turn

After you submit your move, the computer will automatically generate its own choice. The winner of the round is decided instantly based on classic Rock–Paper–Scissors rules:

  • Rock beats Scissors
  • Scissors beats Paper
  • Paper beats Rock

3. Track Your Score

A built-in counter keeps track of your results:

  • Wins
  • Loses
  • Ties

Watch your score change after every round!

Restart Anytime

When you're ready to start fresh, just press Restart. This will reset the counter and allow you to begin a new set of rounds.
